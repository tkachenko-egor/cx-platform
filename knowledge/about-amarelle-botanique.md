# About Amarelle Botanique

*Effective 1 March 2026. General brand background for answering non-order questions.*

## Who we are

Amarelle Botanique is a botanical beauty brand founded in 2014 in Lyon, France, by a small team of
formulators who started out making skincare for a local apothecary. We design face care, body care,
hair care, fragrance and makeup around plant-derived actives, and we publish the full INCI
ingredient list on every product page and carton.

We are still independently owned. We are not part of a larger beauty group, and we do not
white-label or manufacture for other brands.

## Where we operate

Amarelle Botanique sells online only, at amarelle-botanique.example, and ships to 28 countries
across Europe and beyond (see the shipping and delivery document for the full list and delivery
options). We do not operate physical retail boutiques. We are occasionally available as a
guest brand in select department-store beauty halls; these appearances are announced on our
social channels and are not a permanent retail presence.

Our formulation and blending workshop is in Lyon, France. Packaging and fulfilment run from a
single warehouse in the same region, which is why shipping estimates in the shipping document
start from dispatch rather than order date.

## Sourcing and formulation philosophy

We favour plant-derived actives over synthetic equivalents wherever the plant version performs
comparably, and we say so plainly rather than implying a formula is "all natural" when it is not.
Botanicals are sourced through a small number of long-term supplier relationships rather than spot
markets, which is slower and more expensive but keeps a batch's characteristics traceable to a
harvest.

We reformulate slowly and deliberately. A product's formula does not change without a new batch
number and a note on the product page describing what changed and why.

## Sustainability commitments

- Refillable packaging where the format allows it (see the choosing-the-right-product document for
  how refills work).
- Recyclable outer cartons, printed with soy-based ink, containing no more packaging than the
  product needs to survive transit.
- No animal testing, anywhere, at any stage, including where local law would permit it.
- Botanical waste from the Lyon workshop is composted or supplied to a regional biogas facility
  rather than sent to landfill.

We do not currently publish a full lifecycle carbon accounting for each product. Where a customer
asks for figures we do not have, say so plainly rather than approximating.

## Company values, in short

Plant-first formulation, ingredient transparency, and treating a reported reaction as a safety
matter rather than a customer-service inconvenience. These three show up throughout every other
policy document in this knowledge base, and none of them are negotiable in service of a sale.
