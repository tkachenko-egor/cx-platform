# Amarelle Botanique — product safety and ingredients

*Effective 1 March 2026. This document is for customer advisors and does not constitute medical advice.*

## Reporting a skin reaction

Any reported reaction is treated as a product safety case, no matter how mild. Advisors must not assess, diagnose or explain the reaction, and must not speculate about which ingredient caused it.

Collect four things: the product name, the batch number printed on the packaging, what the customer experienced, and when it started relative to first use. Then approve the return and escalate to the product safety team.

If the customer describes swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat, difficulty breathing, blistering, or a reaction that is spreading or worsening, advise them to seek medical attention without delay and escalate as urgent. Do not continue with return processing before saying this.

## What we cannot advise on

- Whether a product is safe for a specific medical condition, medication or skin treatment.
- Whether a product is suitable during pregnancy or breastfeeding.
- Whether an ingredient will trigger a known allergy.
- Anything requiring a diagnosis.

In all of these cases, provide the full ingredient list and recommend the customer speak to their doctor, dermatologist or pharmacist. The ingredient list, in INCI format, is printed on every product page and on the carton.

## Patch testing

We recommend a patch test before first use of any leave-on product, particularly for sensitive skin. Apply a small amount to the inner forearm, leave for 24 hours, and do not use the product if any redness or irritation appears.

## Essential oils and fragrance allergens

Some formulas contain essential oils, which are listed individually where required. Twenty-six fragrance allergens are declared by name on the ingredient list whenever they exceed regulatory thresholds. Products flagged as containing essential oils are not recommended during pregnancy without medical advice.

## Shelf life and period after opening

Two dates matter. The printed expiry date applies to unopened products. The period-after-opening symbol, an open jar marked 6M, 12M or 24M, indicates how long the product stays at its best once opened.

| Product type | Typical PAO | Signs it should be discarded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face serums and creams | 6–12 months | Colour change, separation, altered smell |
| Body and hair products | 12–24 months | Separation, thinning, altered smell |
| Mascara and liquid eye products | 6 months | Any change; discard on schedule regardless |
| Fragrance | 24–36 months | Darkening, sharp or sour top notes |

Natural formulas contain fewer synthetic preservatives, so colour and scent may vary slightly between batches. Variation on its own is not a defect. A change within a single bottle over time may be.

## Animal testing and certification

No Amarelle Botanique product is tested on animals. Products marked vegan contain no animal-derived ingredients; unmarked products may contain beeswax, honey or milk proteins.
