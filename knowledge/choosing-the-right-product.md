# Amarelle Botanique — choosing the right product

*Effective 1 March 2026. Guidance for matching products to skin profiles.*

## Skin profiles

| Profile | How it presents | Look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | Comfortable, few concerns | Light hydration, radiance |
| Dry | Tightness, flaking, dullness | Rich textures, oils, nourishing botanicals |
| Oily | Shine, visible pores, congestion | Purifying, light gel textures, non-comedogenic |
| Combination | Oily centre, drier cheeks | Balancing formulas, lighter textures overall |
| Sensitive | Redness, stinging, reactivity | Soothing, fragrance-free, minimal ingredient lists |
| Mature | Loss of firmness, fine lines | Firming, replenishing, richer night textures |

Skin profile is not fixed. It shifts with season, climate, hormones and age. Customers in cold or heated environments often move toward dry in winter.

## Building a routine

- Cleanse, treat, moisturise. Anything else is optional.
- Introduce one new product at a time and wait two weeks. Introducing three at once makes a reaction impossible to trace.
- Apply from lightest to richest texture.
- Sun protection goes last in the morning routine.

## Fragrance families

| Family | Character | Typical notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Floral | Soft, familiar, versatile | Rose, linden, elderflower |
| Fresh | Bright, clean, energising | Citrus, green leaves, sea notes |
| Woody | Warm, grounded, lasting | Cedar, vetiver, chicory root |
| Gourmand | Sweet, comforting | Almond, vanilla, honey |

Eau de parfum holds roughly 15–20% concentration and lasts most of the day. Eau de toilette holds roughly 5–15% and is lighter, better suited to warm weather. Scent develops differently on each person, so a sample is always worth trying before a full bottle.

## Refills

Products marked refillable have a matching refill pouch or cartridge, identified by an AB-RF reference. Refills use around 80% less plastic than a new container and cost less than the original. A refill only fits the container of the same product; it cannot be transferred between products or sizes.
