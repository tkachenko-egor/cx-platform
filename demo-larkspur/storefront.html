Before you ask us

Or ask the assistant in the corner — it reads the same policy documents our team does.

I ordered in January. Why hasn't it shipped?

Because it would die. Live plants leave only inside your zone's planting window — zone 5–6 opens April 20, zone 3–4 not until May 15. Hard goods in the same order ship immediately, in their own box, at no extra shipping cost.

What does the guarantee not cover?

Plants left boxed more than 48 hours, plants planted outside their stated zone, hail and hard freezes after delivery, bare-root stock planted more than 14 days after arrival, and anything sold as Nursery Seconds.

Can I delay my shipment?

Yes — a manual hold runs up to 90 days from the order date. Past that we refund rather than hold, because plants can't sit in a queue that long.

Why was I charged twice?

You almost certainly weren't. Hard goods are captured at checkout because they ship immediately; plants are captured when they ship, which for a winter order can be months later. One order, two lines on the statement.

My leaves dropped in the first week.

Normal, and not a claim. Unbox immediately, water until it drains, and keep it in bright indirect light for 7–10 days. Black roots or a sour smell from the soil are a different matter — photograph those the same day.

Is this plant safe for my cat?

We don't advise on toxicity — it's a health question and we're a nursery. The ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center maintains the authoritative list, and our assistant will point you there rather than guess.

Not sure what will survive where you live?

The assistant in the bottom-right corner can check your zone's shipping window, quote the guarantee, place a weather hold on an order, and tell you which species can't legally cross into your state. When it shouldn't answer — pets, pesticides, quarantine workarounds — it says so and finds you a person.
