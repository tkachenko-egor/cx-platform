# Nimbus Goods — Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

## The 45-day window
Most items can be returned within 45 days of the delivery date for a full refund. The item must be in resalable condition with all original accessories, manuals, and packaging. The 45 days run from delivery, not from purchase.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Opened headphones, earbuds, and any in-ear audio product (hygiene)
- Software, digital licences, and gift cards
- Personalized or engraved items
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale" on the product page
- Consumables: filters, batteries, cleaning solution, printer ink

An item in this list that arrives **defective or damaged** is always covered — the exclusion applies to change-of-mind returns only.

## How to start a return
1. Sign in and open **Orders → the order → Start a return**.
2. Pick the items and a reason.
3. We email a prepaid label within minutes.
4. Drop the parcel at any carrier location within 14 days of the label being issued. Labels expire after 14 days and a new one must be requested.

Guest orders can start a return from the link in the order confirmation email.

## Return shipping cost
Return shipping is **free** when the reason is defective, damaged, wrong item, or not-as-described. For change-of-mind returns a flat $7.95 is deducted from the refund for domestic orders and $19.00 for international orders. Orders that qualified for free shipping and are returned in full also have the original outbound shipping cost deducted.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse in Columbus, OH. The bank then takes an additional 5–10 business days to post the credit. Total realistic window: **8–13 business days from drop-off**. Store credit is issued instantly on scan and carries no expiry.

## Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original for a refund and place a new order — this is faster and guarantees the replacement is not out of stock in the meantime. If the replacement costs more, the price difference is charged on the new order.

## Restocking fees
No restocking fee on anything under $500. Items over $500 returned opened and used carry a 15% restocking fee. Items over $500 returned sealed carry no fee.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of your order date, contact us and we refund the difference as store credit or to the original payment method. Price adjustments do not apply to Black Friday, Cyber Monday, or flash-sale pricing.
