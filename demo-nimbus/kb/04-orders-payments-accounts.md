# Nimbus Goods — Orders, Payment & Accounts

## Order statuses
- **Pending** — payment authorized, not yet picked. Fully cancellable and editable.
- **Processing** — being picked and packed. Address changes still possible; cancellation possible but not guaranteed.
- **Shipped** — tracking issued. No changes; redirect through the carrier.
- **Delivered** — the 45-day return window starts here.
- **On hold** — usually a payment verification or an address the carrier rejected. We email within 1 business day; the order is held for 5 days before auto-cancellation.

## Cancelling an order
Self-serve cancellation is available from **Orders → Cancel** while the status is Pending or Processing. Once Shipped, refuse delivery or start a standard return. Refused deliveries are refunded on arrival back at the warehouse, minus nothing — refusal is treated as a defect-free return with free return shipping.

## Payment methods
Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Nimbus store credit. We accept one card plus store credit on a single order; we cannot split across two cards. Purchase orders and net-30 terms are available for verified business accounts spending over $5,000/year — apply through the Business Accounts page.

## When you are charged
Placing an order creates an **authorization**, not a charge. The card is captured when the order ships. For split shipments each box is captured separately, which is why a single order can show as two charges on a statement. An authorization that is not captured drops off automatically after 7 days; we cannot force a bank to release it faster.

## Failed payments
The three usual causes are a billing address that does not match the card on file, an international-transaction block, and insufficient available credit due to a prior pending authorization. Update payment from **Orders → the order → Update payment**; the order is held 5 days.

## Discount codes
One promotional code per order. Codes do not stack with each other but do stack with store credit and with the free-shipping threshold. A code forgotten at checkout can be applied retroactively within 48 hours of the order — contact support with the order number.

## Accounts
Account deletion is self-serve under **Settings → Privacy → Delete account**; it completes within 30 days and is irreversible. Order records are retained for 7 years for tax purposes even after deletion, in de-identified form. Data export (orders, addresses, saved payment metadata) is available from the same screen and arrives by email within 72 hours.

## Contacting a human
Live chat and email are staffed 7am–10pm ET, seven days. Phone support is 9am–6pm ET on weekdays at 1-800-NIMBUS-1. Median first-response time on email is under 4 hours. There is no support on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, or New Year's Day.
