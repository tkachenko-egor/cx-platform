# Nimbus Goods — Shipping & Delivery Policy

## Where we ship
Nimbus Goods ships to all 50 US states, Canada, the UK, and the EU. We do not currently ship to Australia, New Zealand, or Japan. We cannot ship lithium-battery products (power banks, e-scooters, cordless vacuums) to Alaska, Hawaii, or any international destination — these are ground-only within the contiguous US.

## Shipping options and cost

| Method | Delivery window | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Ground | 4–7 business days | Free over $49, otherwise $6.95 | Contiguous US only |
| Nimbus Express | 2 business days | $14.95 | Order by 1pm ET for same-day dispatch |
| Overnight | Next business day | $29.95 | Order by 11am ET, weekdays only |
| Canada Standard | 7–12 business days | $18.00 | Duties collected at checkout |
| UK / EU Standard | 8–14 business days | $22.00 | Duties collected at checkout |

Free shipping thresholds are calculated on the merchandise subtotal after discounts and before tax.

## Processing time
Orders placed before 1:00pm ET on a business day are picked and packed the same day. Orders placed after 1pm ET, on weekends, or on a US federal holiday begin processing the next business day. Personalized or engraved items add 2 business days to processing regardless of shipping method.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed within 24 hours of dispatch. Tracking can take up to 12 hours to show movement after the label is created — a "label created, not yet in transit" status during that window is normal and does not indicate a problem.

## Late, lost, and damaged shipments
- A domestic package is considered **delayed** at 3 business days past the estimated delivery date and **lost** at 10 business days past. We will reship or refund a lost order at no cost.
- If a package arrives damaged, photograph the box and contents before discarding anything and contact us within 7 days. We ship a replacement immediately; no return of the damaged item is required for orders under $150.
- If tracking says "Delivered" but the package is missing, wait 48 hours (carriers frequently scan early), check with neighbours and building reception, then contact us. We file a carrier claim and reship.

## Address changes
An address can be changed only while the order status is "Processing." Once the status reads "Shipped," the address is locked and the package must be redirected through the carrier's own portal, which may incur a carrier fee.

## Split shipments
Multi-item orders may ship in separate boxes at no extra charge. Each box gets its own tracking number. A partial delivery is not a sign of a missing item — check all tracking numbers on the order before contacting support.
