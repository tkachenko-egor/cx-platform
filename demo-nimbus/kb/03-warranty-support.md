# Nimbus Goods — Warranty & Product Support

## Nimbus Standard Warranty
Every product sold by Nimbus Goods carries a **2-year limited warranty** against manufacturing defects, starting on the delivery date. This runs alongside — and does not replace — any manufacturer warranty, and alongside statutory consumer rights in the UK and EU.

Covered: component failure, dead pixels above the manufacturer threshold, battery capacity below 70% of rated within 2 years, connectivity hardware failure, seams and hinges separating under normal use.

Not covered: liquid damage, cracked screens from drops, cosmetic scuffs, damage from third-party chargers or non-approved parts, normal wear on cables and ear tips, and any unit with a removed or altered serial number.

## Nimbus Care+ (optional, paid)
Care+ extends coverage to 4 years and adds two accidental-damage claims per year with a $39 service fee per claim. Care+ must be purchased within 30 days of the original order and is transferable if the product is gifted or sold. It is refundable pro-rata if cancelled.

## Filing a warranty claim
1. Have the order number and the product serial number ready. The serial is on the underside of the device, inside the battery door, or in Settings → About.
2. Open **Orders → the order → Report a problem**, or contact support.
3. Our team runs a short diagnostic conversation first — roughly 40% of claims are resolved without a replacement.
4. If a replacement is approved we ship it before the faulty unit is returned, holding a $1 authorization on the card. The return label is included in the box.

Typical end-to-end claim time: 3–5 business days.

## Common troubleshooting

**Device will not power on.** Charge for a full 30 minutes on the supplied cable and adapter before assuming failure — deeply discharged lithium cells show no LED for the first several minutes. Then try a different outlet, then a different cable.

**Bluetooth device will not pair.** Forget the device in your phone's Bluetooth settings, hold the pairing button for 10 seconds until the LED alternates, and pair fresh. A device already connected to a second phone or laptop nearby will not appear as available.

**Battery drains faster than rated.** Rated battery life assumes 50% volume and default settings. Run one full discharge-to-recharge cycle before judging. Capacity below 70% of rated within the warranty period is a covered defect.

**Firmware update fails.** Keep the device on the charger and within 1 metre of the phone throughout. A failed update never bricks a Nimbus device — power-cycle and retry.
