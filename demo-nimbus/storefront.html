Questions we get a lot

Or just ask the assistant in the corner — it reads the same policy docs.

How long does a refund actually take?

Three business days from the moment your return lands at our Columbus warehouse, then five to ten more for your bank to post it. Store credit is instant on scan.

Can I return opened headphones?

Not for change of mind — hygiene rules. If they arrived defective or damaged, that exclusion doesn't apply and return shipping is free.

Do you ship internationally?

Canada, the UK, and the EU. Not Australia, New Zealand, or Japan yet. Lithium-battery products are contiguous-US ground only.

Can I change my delivery address?

Only while the order still reads "Processing." Once it ships, the address is locked and you'll need the carrier's redirect portal.

Why were I charged twice for one order?

You probably weren't. Multi-item orders can ship in separate boxes, and each box is captured separately — two charges, one total.

What's Nimbus Care+?

Four years of coverage plus two accidental-damage claims a year at $39 each. Buy it within 30 days of the order; it's transferable and refundable pro-rata.

Not sure which one to get?

The assistant in the bottom-right corner can search the live catalog and quote the actual policy — it isn't guessing, and it will hand you to a person the moment it should.
